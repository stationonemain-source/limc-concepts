The tracks

Road, drag, off-road, karting and drift, engineered as one destination. Pick a discipline; every one of them earns its place.

Longhorn International Speedway

A Grade I road circuit engineered for professional racing, manufacturer testing and high-performance events, drawn in the tradition of the great international circuits.

  • 3.9 mi FIA Grade I licensed
  • ~5 mi of road circuits
  • Tilke designed

Area22 Dragway

A next-generation quarter-mile built to NHRA and IHRA sanctioning standards: national-caliber events, private testing and grassroots programs, year round.

  • 1/4 mi drag strip
  • NHRA + IHRA standards
  • Night racing under lights

Blackland Off-Road

A motocross park and arena for competitive racing and rider training, beside customizable proving grounds for trucks, side-by-sides, ATVs and emerging off-road technology.

  • MX park and arena
  • Proving grounds for manufacturers
  • Real-terrain testing

Longhorn Karting Complex

The gateway into professional motorsports: a quarter-mile karting circuit for every age, plus Battle Racing, the interactive karting attraction built with Emotional Ideas.

  • 1/4 mi karting circuit
  • All ages driver development
  • Battle Racing attraction

Longhorn Drift Park

Technical corners, high-speed transitions and spectator zones built for competition, exhibitions, training and the nighttime energy of modern drift culture.

  • 2 mi drifting circuit
  • Night sessions
  • Competition ready

More than a racetrack

The campus is designed so a race weekend never has to end: live beside the circuit, dine over it, and put your kids in a race academy on it.

MotorVille

Car condos over the road circuit

Luxury two-story automotive suites from roughly 1,300 to 2,500 square feet: secure vehicle storage, entertaining space and office functionality with premium views of the circuit, built for collectors, race teams and businesses.

Hospitality

Blacklands Chophouse and Vic's Bourbon Bar

Boutique hotels overlooking the campus, a refined Texas chophouse, and an after-the-race bourbon bar with craft cocktails and live entertainment. The Paddock Shops add retail, dining and fan activations built around car culture.

The Paddock Shops

Retail built around car culture

Performance brands, dining and fan activations in one district, anchored by Motorville Pro Shops and Vic's Motohaus Performance Parts: paint and body, motorsports prep, custom builds, Dynojet tuning and vehicle diagnostics.

Texas Race Academy

Training the next generation

Racers, engineers, mechanics and media, trained on campus, with veterans career programs and youth initiatives. Grade 1 Racing simulators and immersive karting keep fans and families in the seat between race days.

Motorsports is big business

LIMC is a diversified platform across sport, real estate, hospitality and technology. Four revenue engines share one piece of land.

Facilities and event operations

Revenue tied directly to venue activity: sanctioned race weekends, private track rentals, karting and year-round programming across five disciplines.

Commercial real estate

Ground leases, tenant rents and mixed-use development across the campus, with part of the property sitting inside a federal Opportunity Zone.

Automotive R&D and testing

Long-term proving-ground partnerships with manufacturers and technology companies: land leases plus consistent track revenue, quietly compounding.

Education and workforce

Texas Race Academy and veterans career programs create jobs and a talent pipeline for the industry the campus itself will need.
